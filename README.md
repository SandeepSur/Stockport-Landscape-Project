# Stockport Landscape Project

A modern, responsive, high-performance website for **Stockport Landscape Project** — premier garden design, landscaping, and grounds maintenance specialists serving Stockport, Greater Manchester, and Cheshire.

## 🌿 Live Preview & Overview

The project features a full luxury website layout built with semantic HTML5, modern CSS3, and vanilla JavaScript:

1. **Hero Banner (`#home`)**:
   - Cinematic fullscreen video background (`hero-banner-video.mp4`) with smooth autoplay, looping, and fallback poster.
   - Frosted trust badge pill: *Local · Stockport's Garden Specialists*.
   - Headline & call-to-action button: *Get a Free Quote →*.
   - Star rating strip: *★★★★★ Rated 4.9 by Stockport Homeowners*.
   - Video playback & mute controls, plus drag-and-drop video previewer.

2. **Values & Story Section (`#about`)**:
   - 3-column top values bar with perpetual floating icon animations.
   - "Dedicated to Keeping Green Spaces Beautiful" story spotlight with landscape photography.

3. **Services Grid (`#services`)**:
   - 6 interactive service cards (Garden Design, Full Landscaping, Planting, Hardscaping, Lawn Care, Maintenance).
   - Scroll-triggered fade-up animation re-triggerable on scroll.

4. **Why Homeowners Choose Us (`#why-us`)**:
   - 3-column Bento grid with featured center mower photo card and *Get My Estimate* action pill.
   - Trust highlights: *15+ Years Strong*, *Licensed & Certified*, *90+ Projects Done*, *We Show Up*.

5. **Client Testimonials & Stats (`#stats`)**:
   - Luxury client stats cards with counting number counter animations on viewport entry.

6. **4-Step Process (`#process`)**:
   - "Simple Steps to Your Dream Garden" roadmap (Consultation, Design, Construction, Handover).
   - Professional guarantee card: *Fully Insured & Guaranteed*.

7. **Natural Outdoor Banner (`#quote-banner`)**:
   - Split accent banner: gardener photography + deep forest green consultation block.

8. **Frequently Asked Questions (`#faq`)**:
   - 2-column layout: "Transform Your Garden Today" callout card with lime CTA button.
   - Interactive accordion with smooth expand/collapse and active indicator states.

9. **Luxury Site Footer (`#contact`)**:
   - Top CTA ribbon with direct call and free consultation actions.
   - 4-column layout: brand bio, quick navigation, services directory, and contact & local service areas.
   - Social links, copyright, and smooth back-to-top button.
   - Dynamic frosted ambient bottom blur effect.

## 🚀 Getting Started

To run locally:
```bash
# Using Python
python -m http.server 5500

# Or open index.html directly in any modern browser
```

Visit `http://localhost:5500` in your browser.
